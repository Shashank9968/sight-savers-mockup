What it is

Low vision means sight that cannot be fully corrected with ordinary glasses, contact lenses or treatment, and that makes everyday tasks such as reading, recognising faces or moving around harder. It can follow many eye conditions.

Clinical wording to be approved by our optometrists [confirm] · Last reviewed [placeholder]

Signs worth checking

  • Your glasses don’t help any more
  • Reading, faces or TV are hard to see
  • You’ve been told your vision can’t be corrected further

None of these alone means treatment is needed. An assessment tells you.

How it affects vision

Low-vision aids do not cure the underlying condition, but magnifiers, telescopic devices, lighting and practical techniques can make the most of the sight you have. Any medical treatment stays with your eye doctor; we focus on aids and advice.

When not to wait for an appointment

Sudden loss or clouding of vision, eye pain, a red painful eye while wearing lenses, or new flashes and floaters need urgent care. Remove any lenses and contact an eye doctor or emergency department straight away; don’t use online booking.
