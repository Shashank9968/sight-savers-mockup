Only in the UAE

Specialist care you won’t find elsewhere in the UAE.

Sight Savers Optics is the only optical store in the UAE offering scleral lenses, low vision aids and RevitalVision vision therapy. Each is assessed and fitted by our own optometrists.

01 · Custom-designed from a 3D map of your eye

Scleral lenses

Large, custom lenses that rest on the white of the eye, for comfortable, sharp vision in keratoconus and irregular corneas when glasses and ordinary lenses no longer work.

Learn more →
02 · Bioptic telescope glasses

Low vision aids

Bioptic telescope glasses that help you use the sight you have, to read, recognise faces and see the board again.

Learn more →
03 · Home-based training · no surgery, no drops

RevitalVision

A home computer programme that trains the brain to see sharper, for adults with lazy eye and reduced contrast.

Learn more →
