Specialty Eye Care

When regular glasses aren’t enough

Some eyes need more than a new prescription. Our specialist team assesses, fits and follows up on every case.

Specialty care is fitted in store, so these pages never show a cart. Every button is Book or WhatsApp.

“My child’s short-sightedness keeps getting worse.”

Myopia control for children

Slow down how fast your child’s short-sightedness gets worse, not just correct it. ZEISS MyoCare, Essilor Stellest and Soft ProAssist lenses.

Learn moreBook a myopia assessment

“I can’t wear normal contact lenses.”

Keratoconus & scleral lenses

Custom lenses for uneven corneas, made from a detailed map of your eye: Excellent-KK, EyePal and i-Shape.

Learn moreBook a keratoconus fitting

“I don’t want glasses during the day.”

Ortho-K overnight lenses

Wear them while you sleep and see clearly all day, without glasses or daytime lenses. Swiss-made i-Night lenses, for suitable eyes.

Learn moreIs Ortho-K right for me?

“My glasses don’t help any more.”

Low vision aids

Make the most of the sight you have, with Ocutech telescope glasses (1.7x–6.0x) and magnifiers.

Learn moreBook a low vision assessment

“I was told I have a lazy eye.”

Vision therapy

RevitalVision: a home-based training programme of about 3 months, for amblyopia (lazy eye) and reduced contrast.
