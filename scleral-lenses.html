Scleral lenses.

Large, custom gas-permeable lenses that vault over the whole cornea and rest on the white of the eye. They feel almost like a soft lens and can restore clear, stable vision when glasses and ordinary lenses fail.

What they are

A new, smooth optical surface.

A scleral lens holds a thin reservoir of fluid over the cornea, so an irregular or damaged surface no longer gets in the way of clear sight.

How they work

The lens vaults the entire corneal surface and rests on the sclera, the white of the eye. There is no direct contact with the cornea, and the fluid layer keeps the eye bathed and comfortable all day.

Why they feel comfortable

Because the edge sits on the less sensitive sclera rather than the cornea, most people find them far more comfortable than small rigid lenses.

Freeform design

For the most complex eyes we use i-Shape freeform scleral lenses by Appenzeller (Switzerland), designed from a full 3D map of your eye so the lens follows your sclera’s exact shape, with better alignment, stability and less edge lift.

Who they help

When ordinary lenses have failed.

Scleral lenses are often the next step for people told their eyes are “too difficult” for contact lenses.

Conditions we fit

  • Keratoconus, from mild to advanced
  • After a corneal graft (keratoplasty)
  • Ectasia after LASIK or PRK
  • Pellucid marginal degeneration and irregular astigmatism

Ocular surface disease

  • Severe dry eye syndrome
  • Graft-versus-host disease (GVHD)
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome
  • Corneal scarring and aniridia
The process

From first scan to perfect fit.

  1. 01

    Assessment & mapping

    Eye-health check and a full corneo-scleral scan with the Eaglet ESP topographer. We were the first optometrists in the Middle East to offer it.

  2. 02

    Trial lenses

    We try diagnostic lenses on your eye and check the fit under the slit lamp.

  3. 03

    Custom lenses

    Your lenses are made to your exact measurements by EyePal (USA) or Appenzeller (Switzerland).

  4. 04

    Training & follow-up

    We teach you to put in, remove and care for your lenses, then review comfort, clarity and eye health.

Technology

Built on precise measurements.

The quality of a scleral fit depends on how well the eye is measured.

Eaglet Eye ESP

An eye surface profiler that maps the cornea and the sclera, the measurement freeform scleral lenses are designed from.

EyePal Mini Scleral & Scleral Cone

FDA-approved, made in the USA, with four designs: sphere, front-toric, multifocal and toric-PC, plus a 10-step lift adjustment for a precise landing.

Appenzeller i-Shape

Swiss-made, CE-marked freeform scleral lenses for asymmetric eyes.

When not to wait for an appointment

Sudden loss or clouding of vision, eye pain, a red painful eye while wearing lenses, or new flashes and floaters need urgent care. Remove any lenses and contact an eye doctor or emergency department straight away; don’t use online booking.
