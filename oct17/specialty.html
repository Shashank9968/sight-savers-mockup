Which sounds like you?

Tap the one closest to your situation.

My vision is still blurry, even with new glasses.

Some eyes have an uneven front surface that glasses can’t correct. A custom lens can give you clearer, steadier sight.

Often helped by scleral lenses: large, custom lenses that rest on the white of the eye. Our specialist will confirm what suits you.

I’ve been told I have keratoconus.

Keratoconus can be managed comfortably. Specially fitted lenses can bring back clear vision, from mild to advanced stages.

Often helped by scleral lenses for keratoconus: custom lenses that give the eye a new, smooth surface. Our specialist will confirm what suits you.

My eyes are very dry, and normal lenses hurt.

A lens that rests on the white of the eye can keep the surface bathed in fluid, which brings comfort and clear sight.

Often helped by scleral lenses: large, custom lenses that keep the eye’s surface bathed in fluid. Our specialist will confirm what suits you.

Reading is hard, even with my glasses.

When glasses aren’t enough, bioptic telescope glasses can help you read, see faces and enjoy what you love again.

Often helped by low vision aids: bioptic telescope glasses that help you use the sight you have. Our specialist will confirm what suits you.

I have a lazy eye that never fully improved.

Training your eyes and brain at home can sharpen sight and contrast, with no surgery or medicine.

Often helped by RevitalVision: a home computer programme that trains the brain to see sharper. Our specialist will confirm what suits you.

My child’s short-sight keeps getting worse.

There are ways to help slow it down, including lenses worn only overnight for clear daytime vision.

Often helped by myopia control: treatments that slow how fast short-sightedness gets worse, such as Ortho-K overnight lenses. Our specialist will confirm what suits you.

Questions

Good to know

What happens at my first visit?

Our specialist assesses your eyes, listens to how your vision affects your day, and explains which option suits you. There’s no need to know any lens names beforehand.

What is a scleral lens?

A larger, custom lens that vaults over the front of the eye and rests on the white of the eye. It’s used for keratoconus, irregular corneas, after corneal grafts and for severe dry eye, where ordinary lenses don’t work well.

Is RevitalVision surgery or medication?

Neither. It’s a computer-based visual training programme you do at home, in a series of short sessions. It works alongside your glasses or lenses, not instead of them.

Who are low vision aids for?

People whose sight can’t be fully corrected with glasses, for example because of macular degeneration or diabetic eye disease. Aids can help with reading, distance and everyday tasks.

Can my child’s short-sight be slowed?

There are options, such as special lenses worn overnight that gently reshape the eye for clear vision during the day. Our specialist will check whether they suit your child.

Is it really only available here?

Sight Savers is the only optical store in the UAE offering scleral lenses, low vision aids and RevitalVision vision therapy.
