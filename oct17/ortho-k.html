What it is

Orthokeratology (Ortho-K) uses custom rigid lenses worn overnight. They gently and temporarily reshape the front of the eye while you sleep, so you may be able to see clearly during the day without glasses or daytime lenses.

Signs worth checking

  • You’re short-sighted (up to about -6.00)
  • You play sport or swim and glasses get in the way
  • You don’t want to wear lenses during the day
  • Your child’s short-sightedness keeps getting worse

None of these alone means treatment is needed. An assessment tells you.

How it affects vision

The effect is temporary and reversible: if you stop wearing the lenses, the cornea returns to its original shape. Ortho-K is also used in some children as one option for myopia control. Overnight lens wear needs careful hygiene, and your optometrist will advise whether it suits your eyes.

Ortho-K in detail

Clear days, without surgery.

Get rid of your glasses and daytime contact lenses without surgery. Ortho-K also helps control myopia progression.

How it works

Custom rigid lenses worn overnight gently reshape the cornea while you sleep, giving clear daytime vision. The effect is temporary and reversible.

For children

Ortho-K creates a peripheral focus on the retina that is thought to slow eye growth. Peer-reviewed studies report a 30–60% reduction in the rate of myopia progression compared with glasses.

Our lenses

Swiss-made Appenzeller overnight lenses, designed from corneal topography with the Eaglet ESP.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Ask an optometrist
Ortho-K lenses are worn overnight. They gently reshape the front of the eye, so you see clearly the next day without glasses. The effect is temporary and reversible. It can suit children and young adults with short-sightedness up to about -6.00. We decide after a full assessment, with regular check-ups.

When not to wait for an appointment

Sudden loss or clouding of vision, eye pain, a red painful eye while wearing lenses, or new flashes and floaters need urgent care. Remove any lenses and contact an eye doctor or emergency department straight away; don’t use online booking.
