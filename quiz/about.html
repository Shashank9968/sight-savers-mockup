Winner, Best Optical Store.

Winner of the Best Optical Store (Boutique Stores, MENA) category at the You & Eye Awards 2025, hosted by VisionPlus Expo and supported by industry leaders including ZEISS.

The You & Eye Awards celebrate excellence in eyewear and optical retail across the Middle East and North Africa. For us, the recognition reflects a simple idea: blend expert eye care with a carefully curated selection of eyewear, and make specialist care accessible to everyone.

Only in the UAE

Specialist care you won’t find elsewhere in the UAE.

Sight Savers Optics is the only optical store in the UAE offering scleral lenses, low vision aids and RevitalVision vision therapy. Each is assessed and fitted by our own optometrists.

01 · Custom-designed from a 3D map of your eye

Scleral lenses

Large, custom lenses that rest on the white of the eye, for comfortable, sharp vision in keratoconus and irregular corneas when glasses and ordinary lenses no longer work.

Learn more →
02 · Telescopic glasses and magnifiers

Low vision aids

Magnifiers and telescopes that help you use the sight you have, to read, recognise faces and see the board again.

Learn more →
03 · Home-based training · no surgery, no drops

RevitalVision

A home computer programme that trains the brain to see sharper, for adults with lazy eye and reduced contrast.

Learn more →

Founder · Optometrist

Sridhar Swarna.

Founder of Sight Savers Optics LLC. An optometrist with over three decades of experience and expertise in specialty contact lenses for keratoconus and orthokeratology, he has helped thousands of patients with eye conditions achieve great vision.

What sets us apart

Firsts, and onlys.

A team of skilled, licensed optometrists, trained to tailor care to each patient.

Only in the UAE

The only optical store in the UAE offering scleral lenses, low vision aids and RevitalVision vision therapy.

First in the Middle East

The first optometrists in the Middle East to offer corneal topography with the Eaglet ESP topographer, essential for keratoconus and orthokeratology.

Specialist services

Scleral lenses, Ortho-K and myopia control for children, low vision aids, vision therapy for lazy eye and Fresnel prisms for double vision, strabismus and amblyopia.

International partners

EyePal (USA), Appenzeller Kontaktlinsen (Switzerland), Ocutech (USA) and Eaglet Eye, alongside ZEISS and Essilor spectacle lenses.
