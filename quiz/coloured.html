Wearing & care

Follow the replacement schedule on the box. Wash and dry your hands first; never use tap water on lenses. Daily lenses are thrown away each night; monthly lenses need a fresh multi-purpose solution every night.

Delivery & returns

Fast delivery across the UAE, or collect from Al Karama, BurJuman or Dubai Investment Park. Unopened boxes can be returned; ask our team for details; opened lenses can’t be returned.
