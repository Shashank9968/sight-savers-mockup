Specialist clinic

Glasses not enough? Start with what you’re noticing.

My vision is still blurry, even with new glasses.Scleral lenses: large, custom lenses that rest on the white of the eye.I’ve been told I have keratoconus.Scleral lenses for keratoconus: custom lenses that give the eye a new, smooth surface.My eyes are very dry, and normal lenses hurt.Scleral lenses: large, custom lenses that keep the eye’s surface bathed in fluid.Reading is hard, even with my glasses.Low vision aids: magnifiers and telescopes that help you use the sight you have.I have a lazy eye that never fully improved.RevitalVision: a home computer programme that trains the brain to see sharper.My child’s short-sight keeps getting worse.Myopia control: slows how fast short-sightedness gets worse in children.

Our specialists are the only ones in the UAE offering scleral lenses, low vision aids and RevitalVision vision therapy. You don’t need to know which you need. We’ll find the right option together.
